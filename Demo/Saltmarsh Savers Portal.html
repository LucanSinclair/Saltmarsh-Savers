Key Insights & Findings

Vegetation Health Status

NDVI values range from 0.168 to 0.288 across monitoring sites, indicating moderate saltmarsh vegetation health with some seasonal variability.

Rainfall Patterns

2023 showed highest rainfall (2344mm) while 2022 was driest (1691mm). NDVI responses show resilience despite variable precipitation.

Conservation Focus

Mangrove encroachment, vehicle access, and maintaining tidal connectivity remain priority management issues across sites.
